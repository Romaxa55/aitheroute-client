# Privacy policy

The [@aitheroute_bot](https://t.me/aitheroute_bot) bot, AI The Route. По-русски: [PRIVACY.ru.md](PRIVACY.ru.md).

Updated 28 November 2026.

We collect only what the bot cannot work without.

## What we keep

- Your Telegram ID, username and language.
- Payments, paid periods and request counts.
- Tokens — only a hash, the first characters, by which you tell them apart in your list, and the name you gave each.

## What we do not keep

Prompts, code, model answers, your passwords and provider keys.

## Technical log

Time, IP address, token id, provider, exit server, request path, status and duration. Old entries are deleted automatically.

## Who we share with

Requests go to the provider you chose, through an exit server in your token's country: it carries them encrypted and cannot read them. Payments go to Telegram or a payment service. Anyone else only when the law requires it.

Questions about your data — write to support. By using the bot you accept this policy.
